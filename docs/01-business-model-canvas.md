# 5E47 — Business Model Canvas

**Entity:** Hasenpfeffer Ventures LLC · **Property:** 5 East 47th Street, New York City · **Category:** Private Cultural Infrastructure — a four-story vertical engine that turns raw talent into institutional-grade luxury IP.
**Version:** 2.1 · **Status:** Aligned to investor deck v5.2; operational vocabulary in plain industry-standard terms · **Owner:** Hasenpfeffer Ventures LLC

> **Terminology note.** The investor deck uses brand-coined names (Volume, 47 Slate, Sovereignty Protocol, Circle of Trust, Dark Floor Policy, Genesis Node, and the poetic floor identities). This document — and the rest of the operating documentation (02–06) — uses industry-standard equivalents (Production Cycle, Project Slate, Confidentiality & Security Protocol, Mutual NDA with Liquidated Damages, Closed-Set Policy, Flagship Location, and plain floor names). The brand terms appear parenthetically on first reference for traceability to the deck.

## How to read this canvas

5E47 is not a club, not a coworking space, and not a studio rental business. It is a **physical IP factory**: four floors (Floors 4–7) in Midtown Manhattan, each with a defined job, operated as a structured 90-day **Production Cycle** (the deck calls each cycle a *Volume*). The deck's investor thesis — *Culture has a supply chain problem. We built the factory.* — is the public positioning.

The defining strategic constraint is **measured output under enforced scarcity**: 100 residents per Production Cycle, 4 cycles per year, selection precedes payment, payment precedes entry. The brand's value is a direct function of the **Project Slate** (the deck's *47 Slate*) — the IP catalog the building produces, cycle after cycle.

Beneath that public narrative sits the **operating layer**: a multi-agent AI system fronted by **Sam, the Concierge Agent** — the single conversational surface residents interact with — and a continuous monthly residency dues structure that funds in-cycle access to services and space. Sam absorbs concierge, scheduling, billing, access, and analytics load so the building scales output, not headcount.

## 2. Value Propositions

**For residents — *raw culture becomes luxury IP.***
- A four-story vertical production system compressed into one Midtown address.
- No-capture environments, private-entry protocol, and a Mutual NDA with Liquidated Damages — private-bank-grade discretion for institutional-grade work.
- 100% ownership of work made on premises by default; the Project Participation Agreement (the deck's *47 Equity Pact*) is opt-in only for projects elected into the Project Slate.
- One conversation with **Sam** handles selection logistics, booking, access, dues, and introductions — luxury concierge at near-zero marginal cost.
- **AI Studio as a Service.** On-prem creative AI agents — domain experts in music, video, 3D modeling, and animation — running on Floor 6's quarantined GPU compute, fine-tuned on each resident's own catalog. Residents direct the studio in natural language through Sam; weights and outputs never leave the building.

**For sponsors — *marketing spend becomes asset acquisition.***
- Sponsors are not buying placement. They are participating in the upside of the cultural assets the building produces — through a governed, auditable **10% Sponsor Royalty Pool** on Project Slate projects.
- Category exclusivity, naming rights, and first-look windows.

**For capital partners — *infrastructure, not content.***
- Private equity takes a position in the factory, not the gamble. Fixed revenue floor from cycle entry fees + monthly dues; compounding IP upside from the Project Slate catalog; multi-location replication via a standardized Location deployment model.

**For media & tech partners — *embedded distribution, embedded tooling.***
- Distribution intelligence and Project Slate pipeline access for media partners; on-premises compute and embedded tools for tech partners.

**The meta-proposition:** *an asset class, not an amenity.* Access is limited. Output is measured. Privacy is enforced. Ownership is structured.

## 3. Channels

- **Invitation & selection** — selection precedes payment; no public-facing application funnel. Referrals, scouted talent, and partner introductions feed the pipeline.
- **Sam (conversational concierge)** — the day-to-day channel for every resident interaction, throughout the 90-day cycle and across cycles.
- **Cultural salons & sponsor receptions** — Floor 5 (Listening Lounge & Member Salon) hosts intimate, by-invitation convening that previews work, softens deals, and builds trust under archival lighting.
- **Project Slate distribution** — the structured IP pipeline operated from Floor 4 (Business Operations & Deal Floor): licensing intelligence, sync placement, syndication, sponsor royalty distribution.
- **Editorial brand presence** — a cinematic, restrained landing experience. No discounts, no urgency tactics, no public pricing pages. The deck is the artifact; the address is the entry point.

## 4. Customer Relationships

- **Concierge-as-agent** — Sam is the always-on single point of contact for residents. High-touch feel, fully automated execution.
- **Committee curation** — selection retains a human-in-the-loop signal; Sam pre-scores and packages applicants; the committee ratifies.
- **24-hour acceptance window** — operational decisiveness is a prerequisite, not a virtue. Selected residents accept and pay within 24 hours or the seat reverts.
- **Mutual NDA with Liquidated Damages** — a binding legal instrument (not a courtesy) across every resident, sponsor, and staff interaction. Enforceable NDAs and liquidated damages per verified breach.
- **Closed-Set Policy** — zero-tolerance prohibition on unauthorized filming or photography. Violations do not get warnings. They get removal.
- **Cycle lifecycle** — selected → onboarded → resident (90 days) → output reviewed → Project Slate opt-in (if greenlit) → alumni. Each stage agent-managed by Sam's subagents.

Blended model: **structural recurring base** (cycle entries + monthly dues) + **IP upside** (Project Slate House share) + **lumpy premium** (sponsor anchors). The Project Slate is the asset that compounds; everything else funds the factory that produces it.

## Strategic guardrails (non-negotiables encoded into Sam)

1. **Selection precedes payment. Payment precedes entry.** No public-facing application funnel. Ever.
2. **24-hour acceptance window.** Selected residents accept and pay within 24 hours; the seat reverts otherwise.
3. **100 residents per cycle.** A scarcity mechanism, not a capacity constraint. Never expand the cohort to chase revenue.
4. **Default to 100% resident ownership.** The Project Participation Agreement is opt-in only, project-level, for greenlit Slate projects.
5. **Confidentiality & Security Protocol is non-negotiable.** Closed-Set Policy, private-entry protocol, Mutual NDA. Violations get removed.
6. **Data sovereignty.** Resident model weights and outputs are quarantined from third-party APIs. Clean chain of title on all IP.
7. **The IP Catalog is the asset.** Every cycle must produce measurable, archivable output. Spectators are not residents.
8. **Human-in-the-loop on selection.** Sam recommends and packages; the committee ratifies.

Both are deliberate operating choices — they protect margin and discretion without contradicting the deck's positioning that 5E47 is *an asset class, not an amenity*. The deck sells the factory. Sam runs it.
